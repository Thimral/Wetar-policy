Privacy Policy for Wetar

Last updated: October 2026

Wetar is a hydration tracker that keeps everything on your phone. This policy says exactly what the app stores, what leaves your device (very little), and what you can do about it.

1. What we collect (nothing)

We do not collect, receive, store on a server, or share any personal data. We have no servers, no accounts and no database of users.

2. What the app stores on your phone

Everything below stays in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your drinks (amount, type, time) and your daily goal.
  • Your profile details used to work out a goal (weight, age, gender, activity level), if you enter them.
  • Bathroom visits you log in the Health tab (time and urine colour). This is health-related information. It is never sent anywhere.
  • Your reminders, quick-add amounts, theme and other settings.
  • Whether you have bought Wetar Pro, and a rating and comment if you give one.

The app asks your device for permission to show notifications (reminders and the progress notification) and to schedule reminders. Both are optional.

3. Home screen widget

The optional home screen widget shows today's total, your goal and a face that reflects how recently you drank. It reads a small summary the app saves on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

4. What leaves your device

The app does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking of any kind. The only times anything leaves your phone are things you start:

  • Wetar Pro purchase. Buying Pro, restoring it and checking it go through Google Play and the Google Play Billing Library. Google handles your payment details and tells the app whether you have bought Pro. We never see your card or account details. Google's own privacy policy applies to that part.
  • Rating and comment. After a number of drinks the app may ask you to rate it. If you give four or five stars it opens the store page so you can leave a public review there. If you give fewer stars and write a comment, it opens an email draft addressed to the developer; the comment is only sent if you send that email.
  • Sharing and exporting. If you export your history as CSV, the file is handed to the sharing app you choose. It goes nowhere else.
  • Links. Opening the store page uses your browser or the Play Store.

5. Children

Wetar is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

6. Your rights (GDPR, CCPA and similar)

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your data is on your device and under your control. To remove it, use "Clear all data" in Settings or uninstall the app.

7. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. The app does not back it up to the cloud, so uninstalling it or clearing its data removes it for good.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, for example to add an optional cloud feature, we will update the date above and tell you in the app before anything about your data changes.
